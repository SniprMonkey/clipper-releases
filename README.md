# Compy Clips

**Built by one streamer, using AI.** Compy Clips watches your Twitch VODs and turns your best moments into ready-to-upload 9:16 clips (TikTok, Reels, Shorts), with captions. Free, runs on your own PC.

Made for **Deadlock**: it reads your matches and finds the fights, kills and outplays. Other games and Just Chatting work from the audio, chat and speech (reactions, laughter, hype moments).

## Download

Get the latest installer from **[Releases](https://github.com/SniprMonkey/clipper-releases/releases/latest)** (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Compy Clips updates itself from this page.

## Made with AI

Compy Clips is made by one streamer with AI assistance: AI helped write most of the code, test it and train the gameplay model.

## Privacy

No account and no telemetry. Compy Clips only contacts Twitch (the videos and chat you analyse), the Deadlock API and Valve (match data, Steam profile pictures, the live broadcast of your own matches), GitHub (updates) and Hugging Face (speech models you choose to download). Your clips stay on your PC.
